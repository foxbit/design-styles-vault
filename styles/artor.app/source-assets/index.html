FAQ

What is Artor?

Artor lets you publish prototypes from your coding agent and share them with your team. Keep versions in one place, collect comments on the live page, and bring that feedback back to your agent.

How much does Artor cost?

Starter: $0 for one publisher seat. Pro: $15 per publisher per month, or $12 per month billed yearly. Team: $29.99 per publisher per month, or $24.99 per month billed yearly. Enterprise: custom pricing. Reviewers are free and unlimited on every plan.

Are reviewers really free?

Yes. You pay only for the people who publish prototypes. Clients, stakeholders and teammates who open links, view versions and leave comments never need a paid seat.

Which AI tools and frameworks work with Artor?

Any coding agent: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Gemini, Copilot and others publish with one command through the Artor CLI. Artor serves Next.js, React Router, Remix, SvelteKit, TanStack Start, Vite, Astro and plain HTML prototypes.

What happens to old versions?

Every publish creates a numbered version with its own permanent link. Comments are pinned to the exact version and element they were left on, so nothing is lost when the next version ships.

Do reviewers need an account?

Not for public links. A public link opens the running prototype with no sign-in, and guest commenting can be turned on. Links shared inside an organization ask reviewers to sign in first.
