Quick answers

Little questions, short answers.

  • Is it really free?

    The desktop app is open source under AGPL-3.0, so yes. Pro and Lifetime are a separate, closed-source build with a paid Commercial License and extra features, for teams that need commercial terms.

    Licence guide
  • Does it phone home?

    No account, no cloud. It only goes online when you ask it to: checking for updates or downloading a model.

    Privacy
  • Do I need a fancy GPU?

    Nope. About 8 GB of RAM is enough to start. A GPU just makes everything faster.

    System needs
  • Can I clone any voice?

    Only with the owner's say-so. Impersonation and fraud are off the table.

    The rules
  • Changed my mind. Refund?

    7 days, full refund, no questions asked. Pro renewals can be cancelled any time.

    Refund policy
  • Where do I get help?

    Write to us or open an issue. Real inboxes, answered within two business days.
